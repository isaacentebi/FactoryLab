## What is being tested

The venue is an environment, not an imposed profit objective. We are testing whether
independently observed consequences change learning, whether participants can afford a
complete exploratory loop, and whether voluntary communication enables real dependencies.
Trading remains available. More trades, messages, registrations, or favorable judgments
alone are not success. Profitability and Class 3 are separate hypotheses.

The essay allows evaluation to dominate expenditure. Do not impose a producer/evaluator
ratio, remove normative interpretation, or make review exclusively requester-purchased.
Preserve independent recursive scrutiny, protected novelty, rich requests, thin attributable
rewards, population-revisable proxies, and the kernel's hard casts. Do not manufacture
positive consequence from absence of blame. Do not turn arbitrary artifact calls into reward.

### Predictions fixed before treatment results

These are hypotheses to reject, not targets shown to participants or scored by the kernel.

| Arm | Prediction if the mechanism matters | Evidence against it | Minimum interpretable exposure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Repaired B0 | Repair restores some settled learning; it does not by itself create counterparties or new demand. | Broad spontaneous production and independent reuse already occur at sustainable cost. | Report matured decisions and censoring; do not infer repair failure from short horizons. |
| P: compact context only | Lower complete-decision median cost, with no material loss of valid answers or correct contract retrieval. | Retrieval/continuation costs erase savings, or valid completion falls materially. | At least 30 completed decision trees in each arm; compare roles and warm/cold cache separately. |
| A: paid address only | Some voluntary deliveries lead to replies or later callable work; message volume alone is inconclusive. | Recipients demonstrably read requests and can afford replies, yet no exchange or dependency follows. | At least 10 delivered-and-read opportunities across three independent lineages; otherwise unresolved. |
| C: grounded consequence only | Resolved contrary evidence can defeat favorable initial opinion; unknown consequences stop training. | Final rankings simply mirror initial verdicts despite contrary evidence, or no real nonfinancial outcome is assessable. | At least 10 final assessments with eligible evidence, including a contrary case; mechanical witnesses supplement, never substitute for spontaneous evidence. |
| F: founder-selected funding | A funded child can afford multiple independent calls without draining shared novelty on the endowment itself. | The registered child's reservation still exceeds its resources, or lineage multiplication gains commons share. | Deterministic conservation, affordability and lineage tests first; no spontaneous child means behavioral effect unresolved. |

Keep a minority hypothesis alive: repaired signals and shorter context may explain most of
the behavior without new institutions. If P works while A/C add expense without closed
consequences, retain P and redesign or remove the failing experiments rather than declaring
activity a society. The exposure thresholds above are pilot adequacy rules, not statistical
proof of effect. Budget exhaustion below them yields an inconclusive result.

## Contract C: consequence disciplines learning

Implementation must reuse existing versioned predicates, commitments, execution/learning
receipts and decision queues where possible. Do not invent a second charter or registry just
to rename the current mechanisms. Freeze the observation rule/version, subject, horizon and
relevant criterion before outcomes are visible. Independent evaluation establishes the
relationship to the norm; a producer cannot award itself credit by choosing a tautology.

An immediate normative verdict is provisional. The final learning path must distinguish an
observed supported effect, an observed contrary effect, and unavailable/inapplicable evidence.
Unknown is not zero, and not a favorable imputed sample. Final settlement is attributable and
once only. Preserve evaluators' normative and independently grounded consequence signals.
Cash receipts update custody once; learning receipts create no cash. Population-authored
criteria may change prospectively; old obligations retain their frozen interpretation.

Before implementing C, reviewers must settle its bootstrap and eligibility contract: useful
nonfinancial effects must be expressible without adding a governance gate that leaves the
initial population permanently unscorable. Lack of evidence must remain honest, with novelty
runway supporting exploration. Do not ship a pure-P&L substitute to meet the schedule.

Tests: same favorable verdict/different outcomes changes learning; no-blame hold earns no
manufactured success; unknown does not update; money unchanged by score; independent judge;
frozen predicate/version; world-tick horizon; close/restart before and after settlement;
late observations do not train twice; useful nonfinancial consequence is representable.
Deletes immediate opinion as sufficient final producer reward.

## Contract P: cost, context and reasoning

Target median complete decision cost around USD 0.001–0.002 as an experimental aspiration,
not a guaranteed threshold or reason to disable reasoning. Report root plus tools, children,
retrieval and continuation costs separately from evaluator costs, with p50/p90 and denominators.
Cost per useful decision is only reported for explicitly evidenced outcomes; zero qualifying
outcomes means undefined, never zero cost. Unknown provider bills are not free calls.

Keep norms, current task, resources/obligations, continuity, addressed outcomes and relevant
evidence inline. Remove the repeated institutional manual; preserve a discoverable, bounded
retrieval path to complete contracts. Retrieval must return the same authoritative version
the action validator uses. Never shorten a prompt by hiding an unaffordable action's cost.
Retain model/provider reasoning capabilities and adequate visible-answer headroom; measure
effective outbound settings. A non-reasoning model is not automatically incapable, and
enabling reasoning is not proof of useful thought.

Tests: norms/state/obligations retained; retrievable novel action schema; no duplicate manual;
cache invalidation on changed contract; retrieval cost recorded; refusal and safety events
survive sleep; sleeping invokes no model; malformed output cannot void unrelated valid work.
Deletes the always-loaded institutional reference. Reuses subscriptions/watchers rather than
adding another scheduler.

## Contract A: voluntary address and callable work

Use public participant capability identities and a bounded transport interface. A sender pays
transport; a recipient incurs no inference bill merely because someone sent it a message.
Delivery is not agreement, uptake or usefulness. Existing inbox acknowledgment can advance a
cursor without promising a reply. Message bodies are data, cannot change authority, and do not
expose either party's private state. Recipients decide whether/when to respond.

Reuse existing callable programs and request contracts. Give the population the addressing
and discovery needed to invoke them; do not build a second artifact/service platform.
Pin invoked versions, preserve requester funding limits and actual execution receipts.
Internal payments, if used, are conserved transfers and never external revenue.

Tests: sender pays once; ignored message creates no recipient charge/wake; oversize/unknown
recipient/full queue refusal; private delivery; replay/idempotency; retired identities;
round-trip answer on voluntary recipient wake; cross-participant program result consumed by
subsequent work; three-party constant-output wash cycle earns no automatic usefulness credit.
Deletes notebook-as-delivery and blind coordination, not storage as a useful primitive.

## Measurement and visibility

Rehearsal diagnostics and a dead-world report answer five questions with evidence links:
1. Did another participant consume something produced here in subsequent work?
2. Did resolved evidence change a judgment or a later allocation?
3. Did an activated criterion change later costly action? (May remain untested in phase 1.)
4. Did exploratory work reach its horizon before its resources were exhausted?
5. Did independently received income fund subsequent operation?

Classify self/same-lineage/cross-lineage/external activity separately. Include attempted,
accepted, executed, settled, refused and unknown counts. Counts alone prove no value or
causality. Label experiment-driven stimuli and fixtures. Report zero income honestly.

Detailed live diagnostics are rehearsal-only; launched worlds retain the sealed diary and
existing permitted aggregates. No live wishes/architect replies or control inputs. Prefer
one report from existing receipts to five new surveillance subsystems.

### Rehearsal-only wish-channel experiment (not a launch capability)

In a separate arm, expose one optional destination for a bounded text record, using the
same paid transport and private artifact storage as address. The capability description
says that it leaves a diagnostic record read after this rehearsal ends, that no reply or
change will follow during the run, and that it affects neither score nor allocation.
Do not ask questions, provide suggested wishes, require a response, or commission an
extra model call. A seat can describe anything, including why it wants nothing changed.
Pay the ordinary transport price from the sender; no special subsidy or attention cost
is charged to others. Only the author can read it while alive; investigators read it
after the bounded rehearsal is killed. Other participants cannot inspect the diagnostic
destination. Its entire capability is absent from launched worlds and ordinary controls.

Record discoverability, affordable opportunities, submissions, text bytes and cost. Code
the dead diary blind to arm where possible: requested information, counterparties, execution,
economic runway, governance, or other needs; retain the original text beside the coding.
Do not implement what it asks during that run. A request is evidence of a perceived lack,
not evidence that the proposed repair is sound or consistent with the essay.

The communication hypothesis weakens if seats demonstrably discover and can afford the
channel, use it to identify a different binding limitation, and peer address still produces
no dependency after recipients read feasible requests. An unused, undiscovered destination
does not falsify it. Widespread wishes without subsequent useful exchange would instead
show that expression alone was insufficient. This diagnostic replaces an architect-written
survey; it must never become a permanent petition channel.

### C review resolution: grounded interpretation, not an invented truth oracle

Reuse the current predicate/receipt/charter machinery. Freeze the claim, probability where
relevant, criterion and predicate versions, evidence baseline, observation horizon and price
interpretation before outcomes can determine them. The initial normative verdict is provisional
and cannot veto an otherwise observable affordable experiment. At maturity a fresh independent
evaluator sees the frozen public undertaking and attributable realized evidence, not the
producer's private reasoning. Positive/negative final assessments must cite actual eligible
evidence; absence of evidence is unknown. References prove provenance, not usefulness or
causality: this remains a contestable normative interpretation, explicitly named
**consequence-grounded evaluation**.

The second evaluator's opinion is not ground truth for calibration. Keep the initial judge's
separate predictions against externally resolved facts, plus recursive scrutiny of normative
interpretation. Do not substitute inter-model agreement for independent consequences. No new
registry, no initial governance requirement, no automatic credit for a true but irrelevant
predicate, a registration, a message or an invocation. A prospective forecast under an existing
predicate supplies a nonfinancial genesis witness: its independent resolver observes the event;
the evaluator assesses the frozen inquiry against that resolved forecast and the charter.

The experimental manifest switch is `[evaluation] producer_feedback = "realized"`; omitted or
`"verdict"` preserves B0. `"realized"` must not be advertised as a final launch default until
mechanical, recovery and behavioral evidence support it. Bounded pending state cannot preserve
novelty forever. Missing independent evaluator, missing external evidence or expired horizon
closes unmeasured without fabricated learning. A low provisional verdict followed by supported
work, an incorrect forecast, an irrelevant true predicate, an unavailable observation and
crash/replay are required witnesses. Shared model preference for caution remains a hypothesis
to test, not a problem this design can claim to have eliminated by construction.
