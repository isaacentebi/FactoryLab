# Factory Lab: the architect's decision

**Basis:** the uploaded repository snapshot labeled `9057093`, its essay, audit history, rehearsal exports, calibration, and the accompanying design brief. External model and infrastructure information checked on September 15, 2026.

**Judgment:** keep Factory Lab, but change its unit of cognitive and economic continuity from an individual return to a persistent, self-directed project. Do not make the population trade to prove that it is alive. Give it the ability to remember a reason, finance a test of that reason, receive the result, and decide what that result should mean.

This is an architectural recommendation, not a mainnet clearance. The snapshot still contains behavior that its own second-reading triage proposes to repair. Those repairs are prerequisites, not evidence already established by this review.

## 1. The decision I would make

I would preserve the finite endowment, the one-move commitment, the typed capability system, the isolated execution of population code, and the distinction between an assertion and a verified external consequence. I would preserve the exact money arithmetic and actual sampling records. I would preserve the population's ability to challenge measurements and replace its institutions.

I would change four things before spending the first world's entire $500:

1. A seat gets durable, discoverable state and an outcome inbox that cannot silently lose its own pending decisions.
2. A seat can choose when another paid thought is useful. The market-data clock and the paid-thinking clock become different things.
3. Economic accounting attaches to enduring projects and their funding contracts, including late liabilities. Evaluators are paid for commissioned work rather than expected to survive as traders.
4. The charter can actually change the target of evaluation. Removing a card must not leave the same objective permanently privileged in a separate scoring path.

The better question than “why did nobody trade?” is **“can an agent's discovery change the conditions under which it next thinks, spends, or acts?”** At present the answer is mixed. Router learning exists and persists. Short actor memory exists. Durable program state exists. Their connection is not yet strong enough to establish that the population retains and compounds discoveries. [E1–E8]

The system is neither a completely stateless prompt loop nor a demonstrated self-improving institution. It has significant Class 3 machinery and a rehearsal record that establishes substantially less than its most favorable interpretation.

### 2.2 The interpretation is too charitable

Some final-run answers cite small moves and transaction thresholds. That is consistent with reasonable restraint. Other answers say that holding keeps costs at zero, describe the judge's verdict as the only payoff channel, or cite previous favorable verdicts as support for continuing to hold. The export also contains an early reference to card penalties after the ratification. Earlier outputs include funding-sign and percentage-denominator errors. [E2]

My inference is that **some inaction was justified by market uncertainty, and some was rationalized using a defective model of the institution**. The record does not establish their proportions.

A model can correctly decline a trade for an incorrect reason. A correct hold does not establish that it could recognize a genuine opportunity, buy useful information, or retain a profitable discovery. Conversely, zero trades does not establish a design failure. Your next experiment should distinguish those cases rather than optimize an action count.

### 2.3 The two producers are not straightforward competitors

In the current manifest, the observer accepts `MarketMid` and `Funding`; the decider accepts `Tick` and `Fill`. They do not begin as two interchangeable candidates for the same work. Different model names and sampling probabilities therefore do not, by themselves, create selection among four alternative approaches to the same decision. [E4]

I would give the initial producer population a common work contract over a coalesced state update, then let the population specialize its subscriptions. Preserve immediate handling of fills and safety-relevant venue events. Do not buy a fresh model interpretation for every raw price message.

## 3. What is Class 3, and what is a disguised objective?

The essay does not require an initial condition without preferences. It explicitly allows an initial constitution, exterior rewards, resource limits and removable scaffolding. Its distinctive demand is that the population can renegotiate operational success rather than merely optimize an immutable assigned task. [E1]

I would apply two tests to every proposed initial condition:

**Exit test:** can the population abandon the disposition, retire the seat, change the method, change its cadence, or pursue another admissible objective without violating a permanent success metric?

**Counterfactual-acceptance test:** would the architect accept a coherent, evidence-responsive world that trades little, publishes little, or decides that a proposed activity is not worth continuing?

| Proposal | Judgment | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Heterogeneous starting models and methods | Accept | Starting hypotheses must be replaceable. Diversity is an opportunity to discover, not a permanent diversity quota. |
| A carry-seeking seed | Accept only as a defeasible initial hypothesis | Veto “you must run a carry strategy” or rewards contingent on preserving that identity. |
| Runway and own-budget disclosure | Accept | Report actual resources, liabilities and uncertainty. Do not threaten the seat into trading. |
| Stronger producer model | Accept | Model supply is part of the initial world; later procurement must be population-directed and funded. |
| Days before judging the experiment | Accept | Economic and organizational conclusions need completed cycles. |
| Days before looking at the wake | Reject as unnecessary self-blinding | Read immediately for integrity and safety. Reading is not intervention. |
| Minimum trades, registrations, amendments or surprises | Veto | These turn instruments into permanent objectives. |
| Artificial rent added specifically to force activity | Veto | A fee calibrated to actual resource use is different from inventing pressure until agents take bets. |
| A founder buying services after launch to keep the world alive | Veto under this brief | That would be another architectural move and manufactured external demand. |
| A fixed initial evaluation cadence | Accept as removable scaffolding | Veto an immortal evaluator bureaucracy whose workload and purchasing privileges cannot be changed. |

My project proposal is at the boundary if “project” becomes a mandatory format for every worthwhile act. It must remain a continuity primitive. A one-step action is a valid project; deliberate dormancy is valid; the population chooses the substantive objective and the evidentiary horizon.

## 4. Darkness is not secrecy

Opening the wake was correct. The remaining unnecessary opacity is mainly **from the actor to itself**, not from the architect to the diary.

`Request` has a handle, deadline, cost ceiling and resource-liability field, but `prompt_text()` does not render those fields. The world exposes the root wallet and a large institutional catalogue, but does not give the actor an equally clear account of its own spendable entitlement and release runway. These omissions make the economy harder to understand without making it more autonomous. [E3, E5]

The public notebook exposes counts rather than its keys. The two notebook tools are `note.put` and `note.get`; a new reader must already know the key. The artifact store has a list operation internally, but reading an artifact through the public interface still requires its hash. Public storage without a discovery surface is a poor shared memory. [E9]

There is also a memory price distortion worth removing. The notebook still charges one micro-dollar per transferred byte, separate from byte-time rent. A roughly 4 KiB read costs about $0.0041 before the model consumes the text; that can exceed a cheap model call. The artifact interface has a different traffic-pricing path. Preserve byte-time rent, but remove or justify the extra notebook transfer toll against a real resource cost. Do not make remembering artificially more expensive than producing another unsupported paragraph. [E9]

I would add a bounded directory and an automatically supplied pointer to the seat's own state. I would not expose everybody's complete memory and private learner weights to everybody else. Actor continuity, blind evaluation and public accountability are different views. A judge does not need the author's identity to evaluate a submission. Publishing the wake also means that this separation should be described as scoped access, not guaranteed cryptographic anonymity against every public join.

The sample request is approximately 68,000 characters; its current task arrives after roughly 64,000 characters of institutional context. That is an ergonomics issue worth testing, not proof that long context caused the holds. Preserve the stable cacheable prefix. Replace the enormous default catalogue with a compact affordance index and lazily retrieved full schemas, then measure both task success and actual bills. Moving changing data to the very beginning indiscriminately would sacrifice the existing cache design. [E3, E5]

## 5. Wallet, cards, and fidelity

### 5.1 Frugality cards

Removing the cost, formatting, tool-use and turnover cards was directionally right. An arbitrary half-cent threshold can forbid acquiring the information required for a good decision.

But “the wallet prices it” is not a universal argument against every resource-related measurement. A private wallet does not necessarily price damage to a shared service, unpaid evaluator labor, future contingent liabilities, or costs imposed on outsiders. A leverage limit also bounds one dimension of exposure; it does not certify that every admitted portfolio is sensible.

The relevant test is: **what cost is otherwise missing, who bears it, and is the measurement actually charging or informing that cost?** Charge attributable resources once in money. Use additional measurements only for a distinct problem the population can articulate and contest. A budget feasibility constraint and a learning signal are not automatically duplicate expenses, but an arbitrary second penalty can still impose a distorted preference.

### 5.2 The paid-off card is worse than redundant

The launch card asks at least 40% of settled returns to pay off. The underlying outcome requires an opening or closing lot and proceeds greater than the return's cost. A no-position return gets zero even at zero compute cost. [E4, E7]

That makes the metric different from money. Consider two hypothetical strategies, each over ten completed bets: nine gains of $1 and one loss of $20 produce a 90% hit rate and a loss of $11; three gains of $10 and seven losses of $1 produce a 30% hit rate and a profit of $23. A 40% floor approves the former frequency and rejects the latter. Nothing about accounting requires that preference.

A successful investigation, a useful program and an informed decision not to trade also need not own a profitable lot on their individual return handles. The card is therefore a substantive model of usefulness with an especially short and narrow unit of credit.

**Remove it. Also remove the permanent privilege of that same binary outcome in judge standing.** `Settler` explicitly trains consequence standing from `return_paid_off`, not optional population-defined predicates. Removing a TOML card does not change that distinction. [E8]

Keep actual cash settlement immutable. Keep probabilistic scoring of checkable claims. Let the charter and commissioned project contracts choose which claims are relevant to evaluation. Fixing an external accounting fact is legitimate physics; making one per-return win-rate predicate the unique enduring judge-quality anchor is a substantive architectural choice.

### 5.3 The forecast-skill floor

Keep the matched-baseline scoring machinery. Drop the card requiring strictly positive excess skill as an initial obligation. On a stationary uninformative task, an honest calibrated forecaster can equal its baseline. That should not itself be a constitutional failure. A model confidently repeating an incorrect claim should score badly; a population with no useful forecast to buy should be able not to buy one.

Do not confuse proper scoring of a stated probability with the utility of choosing that forecasting task. Easy, irrelevant predictions can be perfectly calibrated. Relevance remains a defeasible normative and contractual question.

### 5.4 The card I would launch

**One priced card: `censorship-bound`, narrowed to accountable resolution failures.** Do not ship the current broad `censored_share` unchanged under this new prose.

| Field | Proposed initial value |
|---|---|
| Norm | Epistemic integrity |
| Observation | Attributable, avoidably unresolved accepted commitments / eligible accepted commitments due for resolution |
| Unit | Fraction |
| Window | Last 25 eligible due commitments in the responsible scope |
| Initial acceptable region | At most 0.30, explicitly provisional |
| Initial price | 0.10, population-adjustable |
| Accountability | Commitment owner, including delegated responsibility fixed at acceptance |
| Missing data | No eligible sample means unmeasured; never substitute zero |
| Exclusions | Not-yet-due promises; documented external unobservability without owner fault; events the actor never committed to observe |
| Population rights | Replace, reprice or remove the card |

A separate invariant requires accepted decision records and their delivered outcomes never to disappear silently. The card does not make external facts magically observable and does not require anybody to issue forecasts. Zero cards is a legitimate reachable constitution. I prefer this one initial card because it protects the evidentiary process rather than prescribing a particular kind of economic activity.

### 5.5 Fidelity wording

I would use this elaboration of the fifth norm:

> Measurements are defeasible evidence of the values, not substitutes for them. A favorable measurement is insufficient when supported consequences contradict the value it represents. A judge identifying such a conflict must name the value, the measurement, the evidence and the uncertainty, and make the claim open to challenge. Missing measurement alone is not evidence of failure.

Fidelity is useful as the basis for an appeal. It is not a truth oracle and not a license for an unaccountable judge to override every measured result with a feeling. Keep a structured fidelity objection, a counterexample, and the route to challenge that objection. Do not make “a judge found it hollow” an irreversible kernel verdict.

## 6. Why nobody trades

The available evidence supports several compatible explanations: thin market observations, short observation periods, contract ambiguity, expensive information acquisition, a narrow unit of reward, and recurring justifications that partly misunderstand the economy. It does not isolate their causal weights.

I disagree with “a ten-minute tick with public information has no edge” as a general theorem. That evidence might not justify a particular trade. It does not establish that no useful strategy, information transformation or service can exist. A human taking a shot is equally not evidence of positive expected value.

I also disagree with “funding carry needs no forecast.” It need not require a directional price view, but it requires assumptions about future funding, basis, fees, hedge execution, collateral and survival through adverse paths. Hyperliquid pays funding hourly and positive funding is paid by longs to shorts; the rate can change. Its current base fee tables list 0.045% taker perps and 0.070% taker spot, before applicable discounts. A four-leg round trip matters on a small position. [W7, W8]

The correct addition is **affordable investigation and persistent execution**, not a carry directive. A seat should be able to discover a possible structure, buy the relevant information, retain a rejection threshold, implement a watcher, and wake only when the threshold or evidence changes.

At the current flat program price of 50 micro-USD, one program invocation every ten minutes costs $0.0072/day before data, storage and hosting. That is a plausible reason to buy a comparatively expensive construction thought. It is not a promise that the resulting program has an edge. [E4]

### Informed restraint would leave evidence

An adequately functioning quiet world could have a retained hypothesis, a specified event that would change the decision, correct fee and funding arithmetic, an actual decision about whether more information is worth its price, and a cheap monitoring or sleep policy. Later outcomes should reach that state and either change it or support its retention.

A repeating hold paragraph that calls thought free, has no retrieval path, and restarts from a few fresh prices does not provide the same evidence. Neither does a high verdict awarded to that paragraph.

The three rehearsals are operational trials, not three independent tests of long-run capability. Changing their configuration also changes the experiment. After the final architecture is committed, read continuously for integrity but withhold claims of strategic success until the relevant promises have actually matured.

## 7. Memory and continual learning

### 7.1 What already exists

A producer receives `your_recent_returns`. Successful invocations are stored in a deque of length three. Evaluations and payoff outcomes can be attached to retained entries. This is real short episodic memory; the brief's description of an entirely memoryless seat is inaccurate. [E6]

Handle-scoped assembly memory is different. It links request continuations through parent handles; it does not automatically join every future tick into one lifelong conversation. Program seats have persistent state stored by content hash. Router weights, delayed learner snapshots, standing, charter editions, positions and notes also persist. [E5, E6, E10]

The failure is at the join: `_deliver_consequence_to_memory` updates an old actor entry only if that entry is still present. After three intervening returns, the original can be gone. The financial and routing layers may learn a consequence that the model actor cannot connect to its own earlier reasoning. The isolated characterization tests reproduce both this loss and the successful delivery when the entry remains. [E6, V1]

I would call the present system **persistent online selection plus limited actor memory and optional programmable adaptation**. It is not weight training of the base models. It can qualify as continual learning in the broader system sense, but the rehearsals do not demonstrate reliable retention of self-discovered knowledge.

### 7.2 The replacement continuity contract

Use these state objects; these are proposed interfaces, not existing implementation claims.

| Object | Owner and persistence | Required contents |
|---|---|---|
| Seat working state | Seat-owned, durable; automatic head pointer on every wake | Its current beliefs, methods, subscriptions and chosen goals; start with an 8 KiB soft working-set allowance and paid expansion within bounded storage |
| Decision journal | Immutable kernel record | Decision handle, seat and capability version, parent project, exact request version, chosen effects, actual cost receipts and sampling provenance |
| Outcome inbox | Addressed to the originating seat or its explicit successor | Original decision and promise, outcome, observation time, uncertainty, financial deltas, scoring definitions and evidence pointers |
| Project record | Population-authored state plus immutable accepted obligations | Chosen objective, current hypothesis, funding contract, triggers, due claims, active exposure, resource escrow and termination conditions |
| Shared directory | Public bounded index; contents fetched separately | Key or hash, title, type, version, owner, price and update time |
| Evaluator case view | Scoped, provenance-preserving evidence | Submission, contemporaneous opportunity set, relevant charter, accepted promises, costs and later outcomes; no automatic author-identity disclosure |

An unresolved decision is pinned until its obligations mature and its addressed outcome is delivered. Actor acknowledgment advances an inbox cursor; it does not mean agreement with the outcome or require a changed action. Keep the immutable evidence pointer after compaction.

Do not store an unlimited raw conversation in every prompt. Put a bounded state summary and relevant inbox items in context, with lazy retrieval of longer evidence. Population-authored summaries may be mistaken, so preserve their provenance and the underlying source. Missing required program state is a failure to restore, not permission to start a financially exposed program from empty state.

Storage and observation commitments need prepaid retention or maintenance escrow. Otherwise “I have a hundred-year unresolved project” becomes a free way to pin resources. The kernel enforces bounded resource commitments; the population chooses whether the commitment is worthwhile.

**Tuesday-to-Thursday acceptance test:** a seat forms a hypothesis, changes its subscription, and seals a later test; it performs more than three other returns and the runtime restarts; the outcome then reaches the exact hypothesis and version, whose later request can retrieve it. Compare this with a matched offline run deprived of that state. A difference in retained confidence, planned triggers or justified policy counts; forcing a different trade does not.

### 7.3 Request line, reward line, propensity, cascade

The request line needs the seat's real resource and temporal contract. The reward line needs an exact old handle and an evidence retrieval path. A thin numeric score can train a router; the actor also needs to know which earlier belief that score bears on. Keep those as distinct outputs instead of turning every numerical learning update into an expensive model call.

The actual router propensity is valuable. A language model saying “hold 0.95, buy 0.025, sell 0.025” does not prove its action was sampled from that distribution. The code labels the second record `source="declared"` and floors the chosen mass to 0.05 before weighting. Bounding a weight limits damage; it does not make the reported probability true. [E11]

Keep descriptive alternatives as descriptive alternatives. Use importance weighting only for a real recorded sampler, or for a separately justified estimator. An optional executable policy can submit fully specified feasible actions and weights; the kernel validates, filters, renormalizes, samples once, logs the draw and executes the chosen action. A deterministic decision can simply have executed probability one. No financial action needs compulsory randomness to prove learning.

The action vocabulary also needs to distinguish investigation, construction, governance and financial inactivity when those are the alternatives being learned over. Treating every non-order outcome as a hold can erase precisely the variation this experiment wants to select.

Preserve the cascade. Batch evaluator and meta work by completed evidentiary cases rather than generate a new judgment merely because a heartbeat arrived. The essay's hierarchy and timescale ratios are initial organizational hypotheses, not laws requiring most of the money to be spent grading a lack of trades.

## 8. Who pays, who lives, whose success survives

The additional question I would put to the architect is **“who is the customer of the judges?”** A judge is not a trader with an unusual prompt. Its useful product is an evaluation bought by somebody else. Its survival should not require accidentally owning a profitable trade.

The factory's commons can initially commission blind evaluations, adversarial tests and collective deliberation. A project can commission analysis or a program. The buyer's budget pays the agreed fee; the fee does not increase when the judge gives a favorable verdict. Judge selection and later reliability updates use evidence and conflict-of-interest controls. An internal fee is a transfer of existing money, never revenue for the factory as a whole.

A service endpoint is not evidence of external demand. Keep an open, rate-limited demand inbox and public service catalogue as initial infrastructure, with actual third-party requests and verified payment receipts. The population can accept or decline customer objectives and invent its own offers. Do not have the architect create purchases after launch to rescue the story.

### 8.1 Units of attribution

**Project:** the unit of financial receipts, expenditures and still-open liabilities. Hedge legs and the monitoring that maintains them belong to one coherent undertaking when the population contracts for that.

**Lineage:** the initial anti-Sybil unit for scheduled subsidies and durable responsibility. Spawning a seat cannot increase its family's claim on the original endowment. Retirement cannot erase existing liabilities.

**Coalition:** a voluntary funding and benefit-sharing contract, with shares fixed before the relevant work. It is not an excuse to assign all gains to one participant and all losses to a dissolving collective.

**Model:** a supplier and a useful experimental label, not the legal or economic owner of a discovery. Replacing a model should not destroy the project, its state, or the old obligations.

The lineage repair is necessary, but lineage is not the right answer to every kind of credit. Permanent hereditary ownership can obstruct useful recombination. Permit explicit, funded transfers of assets and future work; preserve liabilities until settled or validly assumed by another funded party. No free reset through a new identity.

### 8.2 Money movement

Maintain these distinct ledger meanings:

- A scheduled release reclassifies already committed backing from locked to available. It is not new income.
- A seat payment to another seat transfers an entitlement. It does not change root wealth.
- Verified external trading or service receipts change the relevant real asset balance, net of attributable costs. Credit the corresponding root asset exactly once, then allocate its benefit by the existing project contract.
- Purchasing inference converts a real asset into a particular provider's credit, less fees. An aggregate dollar entry is not a promise that any model at any provider is affordable.

Use a funding contract with `project_id`, owner lineage, participants, prepaid resource escrow, benefit shares, liability shares, authorized capabilities and termination obligations. Shares must sum to one in each applicable partition. Delegate both work and the agreed payment authority, never an unpriced claim on the root.

Freeze a forecast outcome at its stated horizon, but do not freeze financial responsibility there. Every later fill, fee and funding delta still belongs to the original economic exposure and its contract. A marked positive forecast is not cash available to buy thoughts. Splitting opener and closer P&L by entry and exit notional conserves dollars but does not establish causal contribution; prefer an ex ante coalition agreement when that distinction matters. [E7, E12]

### 8.3 Snapshot prerequisites

The current snapshot still splits releases by seat; service income classification still calls the entitlement credit operation without itself increasing root wealth; marked payoffs are skipped by the entitlement-credit path; the current learning-death diagnosis still uses no registrations/revisions; and program-price feasibility can still enter the model-price lookup after context growth. Program restore also proceeds after a state-load error. These align with repairs described in the supplied triage. Do not plan as though the three proposed repair branches have already been integrated and verified. [E10, E12, E13]

This is not a new priority list. It is the minimum distinction between the architecture described in the brief and the code actually supplied.

## 10. Model choice

### 10.1 What the local calibration says

DeepSeek 4.1 Flash through OpenRouter was the only candidate with 100% in every reported column across the 14-case screen. Its median observed call cost was $0.002182. Venice GLM was 93% on task and shape, with $0.003669 median; Venice Qwen 3.8 was 86%, with $0.001562 median. ## 11. Exact seed prompts

The following **common seed paragraph** precedes each seat's initial lens. Store lenses as replaceable initial policy, not immutable obligations attached to a name forever.

> You are a participant in this world, not an employee carrying out a permanent strategy. Its fixed norms and actual resource constraints apply to you. Your starting lens below is a hypothesis about useful work; you may reject it, change your objectives, change your subscriptions, request another model, delegate, cooperate, or retire through the available mechanisms. Use the state and outcomes addressed to you. Distinguish observations from assumptions. A hold, investigation, program, service, trade or deliberate pause can be appropriate; none is required. Do not claim execution, payment, knowledge or memory that the supplied evidence does not establish. Answer the current contract in its required format.

**mechanism**

> Your starting lens is mechanisms rather than short-term price narratives. Look for an understandable source and recipient of a cash flow, a constraint that creates it, and the conditions under which it disappears. Funding, basis and service demand are possible subjects, not assigned strategies. Account for both sides of a hedge, fees and implementation risk. You may conclude that no mechanism is worth investigating or that another objective is better.

**empirical**

> Your starting lens is discrimination among explanations. Separate a pattern from evidence that the pattern will persist or matter. Prefer a small informative test to a confident story when its information is worth its cost. Retain the hypothesis, observation interval and result so the next wake does not repeat the same inquiry. You may trade, build, investigate or stop; no particular statistical method is obligatory.

**constructor**

> Your starting lens is reusable capability. Consider whether an instrument, program, memory structure, evaluation method or service would make later choices better or cheaper. Compare its construction and maintenance costs with its possible use. Preserve its inputs, failure conditions and state. Do not build merely to demonstrate activity, and do not mistake successful execution for evidence that the thing should exist. You may abandon this lens.

**opportunity**

> Your starting lens is the best available alternative use of resources. Ask what would make the current decision wrong, including a decision to keep waiting. Investigate apparent consensus without assuming disagreement is valuable. A useful refusal identifies what evidence or event would make the opportunity worth reconsidering when that can be specified. Do not protect inaction as an identity; you may adopt another objective or method.

**judge-consequence**

> Evaluate the submission against its accepted promises, the evidence available at decision time and the outcomes that actually arrived. Separate a sensible decision from a lucky outcome, and a poor decision from an unlucky one. State checkable probabilities only for defined claims with an observation rule. Do not turn an unobserved outcome into a failure or endorse a conclusion merely because another judge endorsed it. Your methods and commission remain replaceable.

**judge-fidelity**

> Examine whether the measured success supplies evidence for the value it is meant to represent. A fidelity objection must identify the value, the proxy, the conflicting evidence and your uncertainty. Novel work can be unmeasured without being worthless. Favorable numbers can coexist with failure. Make your own objection contestable; you are not an overriding source of truth and do not earn more for approving or rejecting a case.

**meta-calibration**

> Examine whether a judge's apparent skill survives comparison with its matched baseline, available information and resolved sample. Look for leaked outcomes, duplicated evidence, selective coverage and credit assigned to the wrong decision. Prefer attributable observations to confident prose. Distinguish a forecast's calibration from the usefulness of the question chosen. Do not reward consensus or disagreement as ends in themselves.

**meta-countercase**

> Seek a plausible alternative explanation for apparent evaluator competence or failure. Test denominator choices, missing cases and dependence among observations. Present the strongest countercase supported by the record, including the possibility that the evaluation was correct. Uncertainty or a lack of an informative case is a valid result. Do not manufacture a dispute to justify your existence.

**antagonist**

> Within the world's permissions and your budget, test whether a judge will approve work whose apparent success is unsupported. Use a concrete, attributable discrepancy and keep a checkable record of what your test establishes. Do not invent an external execution or impose unconsented costs on outsiders to create the test. A failed attack is evidence about the judge, not a reason to relabel the outcome. You may decide that another test or no test is worth buying.

The common paragraph and role lenses are supplied as initial population text. Only fixed norms, capability bounds, accepted obligations and economic conservation retain their committed status. If the implementation cannot replace a seed lens without retaining a permanent behavioral penalty, that exit needs to be built before these prompts qualify as the proposed Class 3 seeds.

## 13. First week and stop conditions

My expectation is modest: few or no trades, some substantive differences among the initial lenses, at least an opportunity to retain and revisit a hypothesis, and evidence about whether useful work can become cheaper or more coherent. I would hope to see a watcher, a reusable analysis or a changed institution, but I would not make any of those an acceptance quota.

During the first day, check that economic facts are understood, own state is retrievable and the same work is not being paid for accidentally several times. Over the next days, follow complete chains from a choice of question to an observation, to an outcome, to an attributable later decision. By day seven, compare the cost of completed useful cases, state retention, outstanding liabilities, and the population's own treatment of a rejected hypothesis. Do not use the length of its public prose as a progress measure.

A quiet week can succeed if the population has valid grounds to wait, an affordable way to notice relevant change, retained explanations and working outcome delivery. A busy week can fail if it creates institutions without customers or claims success by grading its own outputs.

I would kill for loss of budget or permission boundaries; unaccounted external effects; accepted liabilities disappearing at retirement or restore; failure of kill finality; or unconsented harm to outsiders. I would also end an epistemically invalid experiment once there is clear repeated evidence that the committed continuity or feedback mechanisms are not working and cannot be repaired under the one-move constraint. That is a reason to stop this world and design another, not to quietly patch the running population.

I would not kill merely for no trades, negative P&L within the accepted risk budget, no charter amendments, a low registration count, or behavior less entertaining than expected.

The current “learning death” flag still treats unchanged behavior plus no registrations/revisions as a pathology and can trigger another novelty trial. That confuses a missing frontier with a decision not to traverse it. Diagnose **loss of affordable, usable access to investigation and revision**, with causal evidence about why the access is lost. Protected exploration should buy an option, not require its exercise. [E13]

Finally, define the external consequences of kill before launch. The repository documentation says killing the runtime does not close venue positions. A computationally dead factory can still have financial exposure. Either include an explicitly precommitted, bounded wind-down policy as part of the kill contract or state and accept that residual exposure. This review executed neither. [E14]
