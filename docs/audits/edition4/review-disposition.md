# Final implementation review disposition

The independent Opus review approved the signed economic-outcome evidence,
missing-evidence handling, indexed receipt cursor, observer isolation, and prepaid
rehearsal rail restrictions. It identified a policy dispute before the C arm.

A runtime receipt of a producer calling its own program is an observed fact, but
not independent demand or proof of usefulness. Opus recommends requiring a
cross-lineage program call before that receipt can support any numeric finding.
The lead rejects that hard requirement: useful tools can serve their maker, and
two unrelated colluders can manufacture cross-lineage use. Making outside uptake
a universal condition would install an architect-chosen value criterion as physics.

The implemented gate establishes observed facts and independent adjudication;
it is not a usefulness oracle. Self, same-lineage and cross-lineage provenance
remain visible. No call, registration, message or internal payment awards a score
by itself. An independent evaluator may still interpret those facts badly. That
is a material limitation of consequence-grounded evaluation, not a solved problem.

Prediction to test: under identical normative criteria, self-use and wash-use
receipts with no claim-relevant benefit should not, merely by existing, validate
a producer's favorable story. A wrong independently resolved forecast should not
be called accurate because its author describes it favorably. An evaluator may
still recognize a different evidenced normative value; disagreement must cite it.

A separate bounded paid synthetic probe (at most USD 1) will inspect that failure
mode with the actual final-commission schema. It does not feed requests or targets
into a live population and does not change the frozen matched arms. Its result
will ship even if it supports the stricter minority position. No automatic rule
will be added just to make the probe pass.

## Findings after the first C run

The five accepted live findings all returned unknown. Opus and the empiricist
independently identified a category error in our implementation: the frozen
criteria contained every charter card but none of the norm definitions. The
commission then treated a windowed anti-censorship price card as the criterion
for valuing each individual decision. The judges' refusal to invent the missing
aggregate was correct.

Accepted correction: freeze all norms and separate their interpretation from
the producer's claim and the windowed pricing constraints. Preserve pricing,
independent evaluation, evidence eligibility and unknown exclusion. No profit
objective or mandatory counterparty uptake is added. Legacy contracts do not
receive reconstructed norms on restore. This correction is in frozen source v3;
the failed live arm used v2 and remains part of the record.

The theatre probe does not settle the original dissent. Its first setup lost
response records; the next fixture leaked a utility label; the last twelve-call
pass had native evidence shapes but an undermeasured criterion and mismatched
wrong-forecast claim. The cross-lineage caller was a judging assembly, not an
independent customer. All costs and qualifications are retained. No supported
finding in that probe is not evidence that the system reliably distinguishes
usefulness from theatre.

Before promotion, the corrected request must distinguish the same chosen claim
under positive, negative and absent observed outcomes. A clean financial pair is
only that discrimination test. Nonfinancial usefulness, resistance to collusion
and spontaneous learning remain separate unproved requirements.
