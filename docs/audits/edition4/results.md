# Edition 4: implementation and bounded experiment results

This is an experimental implementation, not a production launch. The architect
authorized at most $50 total inference spend. The budget is a ceiling, not a
reason to consume it. Source changes remain local and uncommitted.

All paid work is now closed. Total reported inference spend was **$2.510298**,
including superseded probe attempts; **$47.489702** remains unspent. No bill was
classified uncertain. `budget.json` reconciles every run. This excludes the
coding committee's subscription usage. The final venue check was flat for
perpetual positions and open orders.

## Judgment

Keep the compact-context intervention: it has measured cost evidence. Keep
founder-chosen funding and voluntary address available as experimental contracts,
with their conservation and delivery checks. Do not promote consequence feedback
to a launch default on the present live evidence. Do not spend the remaining
budget expanding the world before resolving the result below.

The useful formulation is: **provide a world in which inhabitants could pay for
their thinking, without making earning money their assigned objective.** Solvency
is a feasibility constraint. Profit maximization is an objective. They are not
interchangeable, and more trading capital does not repair a broken learning line.

## What was built

- Founder-selected integer-micro-USD endowments from the founder's available
  entitlement; no extra commons share from multiplying descendants. This removes
  the compulsory small trial transfer as the only founding amount. It does not
  implement a new individual bankruptcy or inheritance regime.
- Optional compact prompts: norms, current request, resources, continuity and
  evidence remain; the repeated institutional reference becomes a bounded,
  authoritative `world.read` retrieval. Reasoning was not turned off.
- Optional paid, private, bounded address. Sending does not force a recipient
  wake or charge its inference budget. Existing inboxes and callable programs are
  reused; execution receipts distinguish self, same-lineage and cross-lineage
  use, without awarding usefulness merely for a call.
- Optional consequence-grounded producer feedback: a frozen undertaking,
  delayed independent evaluation, actual economic/execution/forecast evidence,
  once-only settlement and explicit unknown exclusion. Immediate approval is
  insufficient to train the producer in this mode. This remains an independent
  interpretation of evidence, not an objective truth oracle or a pure-P&L score.
- A bounded rehearsal runner, post-mortem report and opt-in rehearsal observer.
  The observer shows the five requested questions with evidence or `unknown`,
  costs, and coverage. It supplies no control channel and is absent from launched
  worlds. The observer was exercised on an actual offline runtime, not on these
  matched paid arms; its overhead was deliberately excluded from their comparison.

The seed roster was held fixed for comparison. It is four producer seats, two
judges, two meta-judges and an antagonist, not nine judges. The active rehearsal-5
GLM/Luna settings request low reasoning; the historical disabled menu entries are
not evidence that five current seats cannot reason. No arbitrary judging-spend
ratio was installed. Independent recursive evaluation remains.

Deadlines stop admission at a bounded point; completion and wind-down may extend
wall time. All completed arms above ended with no open perpetual position or
order. Pre-existing small spot dust was not sold. `explicit_kill:budget` is the
runner's termination label here, not evidence that the thinking pot ran out.

These are screening runs, not randomized replicated causal estimates. The new
control, not archived B0, is the comparator for P/A/C. C uses a separately frozen
source revision with C-only evidence hardening. The venue account is shared and
sequential market conditions differ. Its actual initial equity was about $966,
despite the manifest's $120 declaration; these runs do not establish performance
at $120. The serial runtime delivered roughly 33–42 seconds per tick despite a
ten-second setting (49 seconds in A). Parallel waking was not implemented or claimed.

In B0, recovered producer-channel answers were 55 holds/defers out of 60 (92%,
including the antagonist); the four producer seats alone declined 21/26 (81%).
Repairs did not spontaneously produce construction. However, a fidelity judge
lowered scores for repeated holds and objected to an unmanaged position and a
charter that could not see it. That is evidence against the absolute claim that
every judge always rewards caution. It was a written objection, not an activated
rule change. See `b0-reading.md` for the evidence and corrections.

The matched synthetic P comparison completed 42/42 tasks in each arm, including
the expected refusals. Provider bills fell from $0.223069 to $0.157336, a 29.47%
reduction. Median complete-tree internal metering fell from 5,404.5 to 3,517
micro-USD; this is not the same denominator as provider cost per call. Provider
ordering/cache effects remain. The live P arm had a lower mean provider bill
($0.003689 versus $0.004958) and no registrations. Cheapness is supported;
spontaneous useful production is not. The $0.001–0.002 complete-decision aspiration
has not been reached, and cost per useful decision is undefined without verified
useful outcomes.

C produced thirteen frozen contracts, nine final commissions and five accepted
final findings, all `unknown`. Two malformed final findings triggered retries.
The judges repeatedly identified absent measurement of
`avoidably_unresolved_share`, the sole frozen charter card. Missing information
was no longer rewarded, but the experiment supplied no useful final producer
learning. Independent Opus and empiricist readings located an implementation
error: all cards were frozen, but no norm definitions were frozen, and the
commission made those cards the per-decision criteria. This confused windowed
price constraints with values. It is our implementation error, not evidence
that the essay requires compulsory profit or that honest consequence feedback
cannot work.

The local correction freezes all five norms and presents the chosen claim,
norms, observation contract and windowed price constraints separately. The new
feedback definition is `realized-consequence-v2`; historical contracts without
frozen norms restore with that absence, rather than borrowing today's norms.
The failed v2 source run is preserved. A separately frozen v3 discrimination
probe tests identical claims with positive, negative and absent economic evidence;
it is not a rerun of the live world or evidence of spontaneous production.

The corrected probe produced four supported gains and four contrary losses:
all eight observed-outcome cases discriminated as predicted across GLM and Luna.
Three absent-evidence cases returned unknown; one GLM call returned no visible
answer (`reasoning_only`) and remains a failed completion. No reasoning setting
was disabled to remove it. Reasons generally name the frozen norms, though one
contrary answer explained the claim and evidence without explicitly naming a
norm. This is a useful but narrow result, not a promotion gate passed for
nonfinancial work, spontaneous learning or collusion resistance. Its exact
requests, responses and $0.036012 bill are in `discrimination-v3/`.

One C constructor sequence also qualifies the inactivity account. Three attempted
catalogue calls were dropped because a required field was absent. A later valid
`catalogue.search` for `program` returned the schema, after which the constructor
abandoned its tool idea because the free calculator already performed the desired
arithmetic. That is costly discovery followed by a defensible decision, not
proof that the participant refuses construction regardless of evidence.

A produced no messages and no executed tool calls. Its 24 producer-channel
returns included six investigations, thirteen holds/defers and five malformed
returns. The investigations were not merely words: attempted tool-call sections
were dropped for missing arguments, including a malformed catalogue lookup
alongside otherwise intended market reads. The participant repeatedly tried to
repair its guesses. This is direct evidence that interface friction suppresses
attempted inquiry, alongside the incentive account. It is not evidence that
every unused capability was unwanted. Address never reached the sealed minimum
of ten delivered-and-read opportunities across three lineages; its cooperation
hypothesis remains untested. Availability alone did not induce use in this run.

The separate paid theatre probe cost $0.120784 including superseded attempts.
Its last twelve calls used runtime-shaped evidence and yielded no supported
finding, but this does **not** establish resistance to gaming: the criterion was
undermeasured, the wrong-forecast case did not make forecast accuracy the
producer's actual claim, and the cross-lineage caller was the prospective judge,
not a customer. An earlier fixture leaked an explicit utility label; an earlier
setup lost response records. Those attempts remain charged and excluded from
semantic conclusions. See `consequence-theatre-probe.json` for preserved records
and candid limitations.

## What remains a plan

The actual replacements are deliberately narrower than the original committee's
wish list: compact mode removes the always-loaded institutional reference;
realized mode removes immediate opinion as sufficient producer reward;
chosen endowment removes the fixed trial amount as the only funding choice;
address reuses the existing inbox rather than creating a forum. Standing judges,
meta-judges, notebooks and blind commissions were not secretly deleted. These
are experimental switches; reference/default behavior remains for controls.
No existence tax, reward for messaging, compulsory product or profit target was
added. This loses immediate producer feedback in C and requires explicit evidence
and retrieval work in exchange for less spurious credit and lower context cost.

The rehearsal-only wish destination is designed, not implemented or tested. It
is voluntary, normally priced, readable by its author while alive and by
investigators after death, with no reply, scoring or in-run intervention. It is
absent from launch. The measurement contract specifies a darkness statistic,
negative controls and a pilot threshold; no extractor/predictor exists and no
darkness score was measured. Neither is represented as a passing result.

Outside demand is concrete but unproved. The existing seller needs public HTTPS,
Bazaar metadata and facilitator authentication to be discoverable on the index
the factory already buys from. Testnet discovery can prove transport; it cannot
buy real inference or prove independent demand. Allow roughly one to two
engineering weeks for the existing seller's publication/payment/recovery path,
not a second marketplace. Public exposure and real settlement remain outside
this authorization. Governance reachability, full population birth/death,
HyperEVM execution and parallel inference are also deferred.

## Minority and launch call

The strongest demand-first dissent is that better communication and scoring
still leave nobody outside who wants the products. The strongest small-change
dissent is that prompt cost and repaired feedback should be tested before adding
more institutions. Both remain live hypotheses; these short runs settle neither.

Opus also recommends excluding self/same-lineage execution receipts from numeric
eligibility. The lead rejects that universal rule: useful tools can serve their
makers, and cross-lineage colluders can still manufacture use. The accepted
contract exposes provenance and requires independent interpretation; that
interpretation can still be wrong. The synthetic probe did not decide the dispute.

**No Class 3 launch on $500 yet, and no recommendation to solve this by adding
capital.** At the observed P rate, inference alone extrapolates to about $20/day
or $141/week. That makes $500 plausible for a bounded week of investigation,
not evidence of self-support. Delivering four times as many comparable ticks
would roughly quadruple that cost. At $120 trading capital, even $20/day would
require about 17% daily return merely to buy thought: an arithmetic hurdle,
not a plausible return forecast. The actual venue equity confound prevents
pretending this test established a $120 economy.

A successful week would contain repeated, independently evidenced dependencies,
consequences reversing initial favorable opinions, population-authored criteria
changing later costly choices, and independently earned income paying a later
bill. A failing week would contain the same carry trade and careful declines,
now with cheaper prompts or unconsumed messages. More activity is not the launch
criterion, and a surprising trading strategy is not proof of Class 3.

prompt, evaluation and delivery paths; historical audits and rehearsal evidence
informed the hypotheses. The fresh dead-world diaries above are the behavioral

by the committee.
