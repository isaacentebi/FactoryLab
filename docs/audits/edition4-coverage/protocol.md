## Change being tested

Tool-only answers that fail validation keep their exact bounded rejection reasons.
Their owner receives those reasons through the existing outcome inbox. The invalid
batch remains unexecuted, the answer remains malformed and its inference stays paid.
There is no automatic retry or subsidized wake. Offline regressions reproduce the
missing order-book depth and oversized batch seen live.

The harness separates a requested tick count from its wall-clock deadline. Omitting
that option retains the prior behavior. This changes experiment stopping conditions,
not the population's charter clock or consequence horizons.

## Predictions and interpretation

The interface hypothesis predicts that rejection reasons are discoverable, and a
later attempt by the same participant can correct that specific fault. Merely
addressing an inbox item is not evidence of exposure or learning. Record retrieval,
acknowledgement, next return and actual dispatch separately. Repeated identical
errors after demonstrated exposure count against this explanation.

The timing hypothesis predicts more resolved early commitments at 60 ticks than in
the 13/14-tick smokes. Keep the existing behavioral screen: 60 ticks, ten assessed
grounded findings, one contrary finding and authoritative bills. No contrary finding
or insufficient assessed findings remains inconclusive; do not manufacture disagreement.
Sixty ticks can close grounded commitments opened through tick 30. Later commitments
may remain pending; the 180-tick governance floor is outside this experiment.

Count actual messages, child requests, registrations, artifact use, orders and income
without rewarding or requesting them. Separate attempted actions, rejected fields,
dispatched tools, repeated emissions and unique decisions. No activity is a finding,
not a reason to invent another institution inside the running world.

GLM reasoning exhaustion remains a separate reliability issue: prior failures consumed
all 4,096 output tokens in hidden reasoning. The earlier 4,096/8,192 diagnostic did
not establish improvement, so this experiment changes neither reasoning nor allowance.
A suggested minimal-effort diagnostic and per-call balance-refresh change are deferred:
the former would consume scarce Venice runway; the latter does not establish timely,
attributable billing. Unknown charges retain their ceiling and stop the run.

This is a provider-substitution arm, not a matched repeat. The runner compares the
new base manifest with its own effective version; its preservation flags therefore
must not be interpreted as preservation relative to r1. Norms, seats, prompts,
endowments and reasoning requests remain fixed. Source is 284c8c2, including the
confirmed-unbilled rejection repair. Controls remain 60 ticks, a 60-minute deadline,
$3, 300 calls, compact context, realized feedback, addressing and no observer.

Prediction: removing known Venice credit attrition should permit more delivered
ticks before a provider-capacity failure. It does not predict collaboration or
successful JSON returns. Provider-route differences confound any behavioral change.
Keep the original coverage thresholds; inspect only after termination. Do not
increase the cap, weaken bill accounting or change models during the run.
