## Open

**T47 — the launch roster's well-formed rate.** Seat 6 measured 84.4% well-formed over 365 live invocations against a card requiring 0.9, with 17 of the malformed returns stopping on `length` with an empty body: `eval-b`, `eval-c` and `antagonist-a` are reasoning models whose budget went to reasoning before any content. What was built is a manifest edit: `max_tokens` raised from 1500 to 3000 on `eval-b` and `eval-c` and from 1000 to 2500 on `antagonist-a`, in both `worlds/testnet.toml` and `worlds/edition1-example.toml`, with a comment naming the cause. Nothing is missing from the code; what is missing is any evidence that the edit works. No test pins the roster's token budgets (no test in `tests/` reads `max_tokens` from either manifest), and the live rehearsal that would re-measure the well-formed rate was not run — `docs/runs/` holds nothing newer than round two, and the three live tests that would exercise the shipped testnet manifest are `network`-marked and deselected in every gate. The row's own second half, "reseat", was not attempted. To close it: run the roster against the real providers for long enough to measure `well_formed_rate` over the first closed window and record the figure, and pin the budgets so a later edition cannot silently drop them back.
