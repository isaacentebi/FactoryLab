# Seat 1: Class 3, cold read of main (Claude Fable)

Read in the brief's order: README, the essay in full, `docs/manifest.md`, `docs/build-spec-v0.8-round2.md`, `worlds/testnet.toml`, `worlds/edition1-example.toml`, then `factorylab/`, `scripts/`, `deploy/`, `worlds/`, the audit tests. Ran `factorylab run --world scripted --events 500 --seed 1` (conservation and verify true, 17k events, a connector registered and fetched, an observation-free but tool-, amendment- and retirement-exercising population) and `scripted-crash` (dies `balance_zero`, key released). Ran one probe script over the scripted world (300 world events, backstop 20, every model prompt captured) to test disclosure and committee composition; its results are quoted below. No live network beyond seven `curl` HEAD-equivalents to public API roots. Nothing was modified.

## Findings

### 1. Broken, serious: the population cannot name what it is allowed to retire, request or train

Three registrable freedoms are keyed by assembly id, and assembly ids are never disclosed to a seed.

- `cortex/registration.py:parse_proposals` refuses `retire` unless `assembly_id` is a registered id; `_learner` requires `assembly_id`; `runtime/compute.py:_invoke_child` resolves `requests[].target` as an id or `self`.
- `cortex/schematics.py:_world_block` publishes `assemblies` as per-kind counts and `contracts` as `{accepts, emits}` pairs with no id (A8). `cortex/request.py:Request` is author-neutral by design and `runtime/loop.py:_producer_step` / `_evaluator_step` / `_meta_step` build inputs (`kind, payload, world, your_recent_returns, your_action_policy`) that never carry the woken assembly's own id. Handles are `decision-N` (`kernel/ledger.py:decision_id`). `Registered` events (`runtime/governance.py:_register`) carry ids, but only population registrations emit them; the nine seeds are registered at bootstrap (`runtime/bootstrap.py:_register_seed_contracts`) silently.

Reproduction (probe): 3,626 invocations; prompt mentions of `seed-observer`, `seed-decider`, `eval-b`, `eval-c`, `eval-d`, `antagonist-a`, `meta-a`, `meta-b`: 0 each. `eval-a` appears 9 times, all in retirement ballots, and only because `world/scripted.py` (the architect) typed `"retire", "assembly_id": "eval-a"` at producer call 100. A real model in `testnet.toml` cannot write that proposal; it cannot write `{"kind":"learner","assembly_id":<itself>}` either, because it does not know who it is.

So "the population can retire any assembly, including the seeds" (README) holds only for assemblies the proposer itself registered and named. Nested requests to other assemblies are reachable only to the registrant of those assemblies, i.e. the essay's imperative "agent A creates agent B" hierarchy (II.I). Fix: publish a contract catalogue with ids (`id, version, accepts, emits, schemas`) in the world block; ids and contracts are the essay's public schematics, and what A8 protects (learner state, router weights, who judged whom) stays sealed. Tell each request its own id (`inputs.you`), which discloses identity of self, not authorship of the subject.

### 2. Broken, serious: there is no kill

README: "one control: kill"; essay I.II, "turns the control tower into a single button: kill". `kernel/termination.py:Termination.kill` is reached only from `runtime/loop.py:_run` when `kill_at_end` is set, or from `_check_termination` on `balance_zero`, `ledger_failure` or compute insolvency. `runtime/cli.py` has no kill command and no signal handling; `deploy/start.sh` launches `funded` with `--events 9223372036854775807` and no `--kill-at-end`; `deploy/factorylab.service` has `Restart=always`. `systemctl stop` leaves the world non-final and re-entered by `resume`; the seal (`kernel/ledger.py` key store, released only by `Termination`) is never released. The only ways the diary becomes readable are the wallet reaching zero or the covenant being broken by reading `runs/funded.jsonl.key`. Fix: `factorylab kill --world W --ledger L` that takes the writer lock, reopens, calls `termination.kill("explicit_kill:operator")`, exits 3.

### 3. Not Class 3, serious: the launching world runs on the architect's cards

`worlds/testnet.toml` has no `[charter]` table; `runtime/worlds.py:manifest_from_dict` then uses `charter/charter.py:seed_charter()`, and `factorylab manifest --world testnet` prints exactly that: `cost_per_return` "below the median of the previous window" (a monotone cheapness objective priced on producers, whatever the trade), `well_formed_rate`, and `forecast_skill` "above zero" over the architect's five predicates. The README says the cards were drafted by the seed population; `worlds/edition1-example.toml` is labelled a schema migration to be re-drafted. Essay II.IV.a: "the first charter is almost certainly coauthored with several agentic systems". As main stands, the first move supplies objectives, not norms. Fix: the launch manifest must carry an explicit `[charter]` produced by `scripts/draft_edition1.py` on the launch roster; have `validate()` refuse `mainnet` without one.

### 4. Broken, serious: connector admission refuses every origin whose root is not 2xx

`runtime/governance.py:_register_connector` preflights `{"path": "/"}` and raises `connector preflight: …` when `"error" in result`; `world/connector.py:ConnectorProxy.fetch` returns an error for any status outside 200–299 and refuses redirects. Receipts (`curl -s -o /dev/null -w %{http_code} https://HOST/`): `api.kraken.com` 404, `api.coingecko.com` 404, `data.messari.io` 404, `api.exchange.coinbase.com` 404, `api.binance.com` 403, `api.coinbase.com` 301; `www.federalreserve.gov` 200. Reproduction (probe, fake transport returning 404 at `/`): registration refused with `HTTP status refused (redirects disabled)`; no vote is held. The population can vote in HTML front pages, not the data APIs a market population would ask for. Fix: preflight on "answered within the bounds" (any status, body within `max_bytes`, within `timeout_s`), or let the proposal carry a `preflight_path`.

### 5. Broken, serious: metas never sit on a committee

`runtime/governance.py:_committee_eligible` counts (a) returns with a fixed payoff on the `verdict`/`exposure` channels and (b) settled `consequence`-channel decisions with a parent (evaluator forecasts). A meta's terminal settlement (`runtime/feedback.py:_settle_meta_consequence`, channel `fast`) and a lower tier's conformity settlement are not evidence, although `_count_consequence` credits them as novelty trials. Probe: `consequences_by_assembly` shows `meta-a` 227, `meta-b` 192; `_committee_eligible()` at the end lists no meta; the three committees drawn (`charter.seat`, `retirement.proposed`, `connector.seated`) are producers and evaluators only. `charter/committee.py:draw` covers the "meta" group only when one is eligible, so the omission is silent for the world's life. Essay II.IV.a: sortition should "resemble to some degree the requisite variety of its stakeholders". Fix: count `meta.consequence` and conformity settlements as eligibility evidence.

### 6. Not Class 3, minor: contracts are open on the request side and closed on the reward side

A registration may declare any `accepts`, any `emits` kind with a schema, a router for its kind (`runtime/routing.py:_build_router`, `_return_channels`). But: `cortex/registration.py:CONTRACT_ROLES` measures every population kind as "producer"; `charter/charter.py:MetricCard.answers_for` admits only the four seed roles or `all`; `settlement/vocabulary.py:SEED_VOCABULARY` is the whole forecast vocabulary (`_validate_params` rejects any other predicate) and `_evaluator_step` requires every Verdict to carry `forecasts` from it; reward channels are fixed in `runtime/shared.py`. A new kind can be routed but only judged as a producer, priced as a producer and forecast about in the architect's terms. Essay II.I: a contract must let "a primitive be picked up against a constraint that did not exist". Fix: let `answers_for` name an emitted kind; let predicates be registered as jailed `resolve(facts) -> bool` the way observations already are.

### 7. Not Class 3, minor: registered observations measure the factory, never its world

`runtime/observations.py:window_facts` publishes 28 numeric fields (probe: `amendments_*`, `censored`, `consequences_*`, `costs`, `fills`, `forecast_skills`, `meta_verdicts`, `noop_returns`, `notional_micro`, `realized_pnl_micro`, `registrations`, `tool_calls`, `verdicts`, …). No mids, funding, time or wallet series; `charter/measurement.py:preflight_card` allows a registered observation only over global closed windows, and `runtime/pricing.py:_decision_share` prices it at `1/n`. The population can add holdout criteria about its own conduct, which is the essay's II.IV.a, but cannot quantise a norm against "an ambiguous field of potentials" (I.I). Fix: add the window's mid and funding series and the wallet series to the facts.

### 8. Unclean, minor: a child's reward trains nobody's router

`runtime/compute.py:_invoke_child` opens the child under actor `composition:<parent handle>`; `runtime/feedback.py:_deliver_returns` delivers only to router learner ids. The child's verdict (it is judged like any return) settles to a learner that does not exist; the parent's choice of target is never learned except by an assembly-level learner the requester registered. Fix: settle the child's score as a fast reward on the parent's handle, or open the child under the parent's router id.

### 9. Unclean, minor: a mixed contract's author is woken to judge itself, then refused

`runtime/routing.py:_universe_for` excludes an author only when its `emits` ⊆ {Verdict, MetaVerdict}; a `("ProducerReturn","Verdict")` contract is sampled for its own return, paid for the call, and refused at `runtime/loop.py:_judged_event` (`return.refused`). Fix: exclude authors from the router regardless of contract when the event's subject is theirs; a producer that wants to continue its own work has `requests`.

## Ruling (a): real or paper?

- **Roles as registrable contracts: real, half-way.** `_assembly_step` dispatches on `emits`, not on a role; `measured_role` and `_return_channels` follow the kind; a producer accepting `ProducerReturn`, a custom `Finding` kind with its own router, and a two-kind contract that binds once (`routing.py:ContractQueue.bind`) all execute in the scripted world and survive resume. The freedom is on the input side; the reward, pricing and forecast vocabularies are the architect's (finding 6).
- **Retirement by vote: real in mechanism, paper for the seeds.** `_propose_retirement` → proposer-excluded sortition → cadence-gated `_retire_assembly`, with pending feedback still settling to the old router (`_retain_router`) and the id re-registrable as the next version. But no seed can be named by anyone who did not register it (finding 1), and no meta ever votes (finding 5). In the 500-event scripted run the retirement never activated at the default backstop (cadence needs 600 fresh events and 3×200 ticks of time), which is correct, not a defect.
- **Nested requests: real, and hierarchical.** Depth 4, fan-out 3, one continuation per depth, children with tools and children of their own, all metered against the root's reservation (`_invoke_child`). Targets are ids, so a requester can only compose what it registered and named; nothing "discovers available primitives" (II.I); the child's outcome trains no router (finding 8).
- **Population-written observations: real.** `_register_observation` preflights the code in the jail against the last closed window, versions it as `observation:<id>`, and a card may name it; out-of-range values are ledgered and unsupported rather than clamped. Over the factory's own activity only (finding 7).
- **Connectors: real in mechanism, narrow in reach.** Registration by preflight and proposer-excluded majority, `connector.fetch` metered and capped, bodies transient and redacted from every durable surface, one parse round in the jail; the scripted chain (`connector.registered` < `connector.call` < `tool.call connector-parser`) executes. Reach: free public HTTPS text whose root answers 200 (finding 4), never the world's own rails.

## Ruling (b): is jail-plus-connectors the right primitive for "what the factory can know"?

The jail is right and is physics, not advice: `cortex/sandbox.py` denies sockets and processes by seccomp, `deploy/jail-check.sh` proves it as the service user, and every read the population makes goes through a journaled rail (`ledger.call` in `_fetch_connector`), which is what makes resume deterministic. The connector is the right *shape* of read primitive: a registered, priced, versioned, credential-free GET is exactly a contract, and admission by the population's own vote is a Class 3 move.

But the set of things the factory can know is still the architect's, on four counts. (1) The world's own market is architect-shaped: `venue.*` tools enumerate only `exchange.coins` and `venue.spot_pairs` (`world/venue_tools.py`, a hard cast); `world/connector.py:DEFAULT_DENYLIST` denies the venue API, both RPCs, every model host and every registered seller, so a connector cannot widen the factory's view of its own venue to a coin, a book depth or a funding series the manifest did not name. (2) Only free, unauthenticated, GET-able text is knowable: no headers, no POST, no x402 for data (x402 buys inference only, `world/market.py`), so priced information does not exist for this factory, while web search exists only as an architect-priced model variant (`web = { engine = "exa" … }` in `testnet.toml`, `runtime/worlds.py:price_table`). (3) Nothing fetched can be kept: bodies are stripped from outputs and memory (`compute.py:_invoke`, `without_connector_bodies`), `memory` is a `deque(maxlen=3)` per assembly, and there is no registrable store; the essay's "memory that persists across rounds" (II.I.a) exists for the learners and not for the epistemics. The one durable population-written text is the registry itself: a 4,000-character system prompt, tool code, observation code. (4) Measurement over what was learned is closed to the factory's own activity (finding 7). So the primitive is right and its perimeter is the architect's: the architect no longer decides which sources, but still decides which market, which kinds of source, how much can be retained and what can be counted. Fix, in order of cost: preflight on reachability (finding 4); allow GET on the venue's public info host; add mids, funding and the wallet series to window facts; a bounded, metered, public key–value "note" primitive so a fetched fact can outlive one request.

## Where an objective still comes from the architect; what is welded on

- `return_paid_off` (`settlement/vocabulary.py`) and the evaluator standing mix (`routing.py:_mix_with_standing`, `consequence_mix`): the realised-consequence signal the essay fixes at the Stackelberg move. Sanctioned.
- The seed cards in the launching manifest (finding 3). Not sanctioned.
- "Useful inquiry" priced as Brier skill on five wallet predicates (finding 6).
- The four roles as the pricing vocabulary; custom kinds are producers (finding 6).
- The action-label vocabulary and size bands (`runtime/propensity.py`), the memory horizon of three, `MAX_PROPOSALS_PER_RETURN = 3`, prompt ≤ 4,000 chars, `max_tokens ≤ 4096`, effort ∈ {low, medium, high}: resource bounds, acceptable as physics.
- The immune organ's response menu (`runtime/immune.py:close_window`): gain up/down, one-window relief, one extra trial. Sanctioned as the incentive-based immune system, though its menu is the architect's.
- The evaluator and meta request shapes (`loop.py:_evaluator_step`, `_meta_step`): two numbers, mandatory forecasts, conformity; the cascade ratio and jitter. Protocol, not objective.

## Does it work, and is it the factory the essay describes?

It works: both scripted worlds run to their ends with money conserved and the chain verified, every mechanism the README lists executes at least once in the diary, and the mechanisms are honest about what they measure. It is not yet the factory the essay describes, for reasons that are mostly cheap to fix and one that is not. The kernel is real physics; propensity, delayed attributable reward, the novelty reserve, sortition, priced cards and the immune organ are built as the essay draws them. But the launching manifest hands the population the architect's objectives as its charter; the population cannot see its own roster, so the three headline freedoms of round two (retire the seeds, compose across assemblies, learn at the assembly level) are reachable only by the assembly that registered the target; one whole judging tier is excluded from every vote; the connector admits front pages and refuses APIs; and the architect has no button. The factory can choose its sources now, but its market, its retention and its measurable world are still supplied from outside. Fix findings 1–5 and the composition freedom the round-two spec claims becomes exercisable; findings 6–7 are the remaining line where the architect's hand shows, and they are the ones the essay would call the demotion to Class 2: the standard by which "better" is judged is still, in its vocabulary, his.
