What a Class 3 factory shows the world

Its schematics are public; each seat's local state is private. So the factory can be followed only through aggregates and public facts: the regime it is in, the goals it has set, the failures an instrument can detect, the charter it co-writes and the prices it posts, and what actually happened to the money.
