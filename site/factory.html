How to read the map

Select a part

Layers run from outside the factory at the top, through the kernel's frame, down to the world at the bottom. Dashed parts are the world, which is not architecture: a venue or a market is simply there. Everything in the heavy frame is bound by the kernel.

Parts marked ● are seats: language-model agents that are woken, answer, and are paid. The rest is the machinery they live in.

Code paths are relative to factorylab/, except those under worlds/ and scripts/.

Instrument / in review

The pathology gauntlet

Does a given world show each of the four failures, and does the factory's price for each one actually bite? The gauntlet answers from the ledger alone: it reads the rows a world wrote and the physics it was launched with, and nothing else. The same checks run in tests, over a dead world's diary, and across many seeds.

Status, honestly

The gauntlet is still being built and reviewed, and no world is live, so there are no verdicts to publish yet. When a world runs, its verdict on each pathology appears on the Progress page: present, absent, or can't tell yet.

Stable failure

Is a lasting violation flagged, does its price ratchet with duration, and does the controller stop cleanly at its cap?

Pass Fail Unsupported: no evidence either way, never a pass

Every threshold is derived from the world's own parameters; the only constant is the 3:1 loop ratio the essay names. A criterion may assert a property of the physics under a price, never a target behaviour, hold rate or count. Alongside these sit structural checks every run must pass. One open question we are checking: whether the factory ever raises its evaluation sampling rate when grades and consequences diverge, as the essay's answer to overfitting requires.
